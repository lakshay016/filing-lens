# R2 session sheet

The facilitator keeps this sheet. Give readers only their task and assigned materials. Keep the reference judgments in a separate file that timed participants cannot see.

## Independent reference reader

Read Item 1A in both original filings for each company. Which changed disclosures would you brief a colleague on, and where is the supporting language? For each distinct change record an ID, a one-sentence description, the earlier and later source heading or searchable excerpt, and why the change merits inclusion. For an addition or deletion, identify the surviving passage and the section checked in the other year. A pair can have zero must-see changes.

Apple originals: [FY2022](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/320193/000032019322000108/aapl-20220924.htm) and [FY2023](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/320193/000032019323000106/aapl-20230930.htm). The facilitator supplies the second company's two originals after selection. Before revealing either comparison, save the completed references for both pairs, record the time and a SHA-256 hash or immutable revision. Keep later disagreements in a separate note. The reference reader sits out the timed tasks for pairs they labeled.

## Timed participants

Use the same task wording as above. Explain the workflow on Exxon if a walkthrough is needed. Before starting, record prior familiarity with each company and whether the participant remembers these particular filings. Start timing when the assigned materials open and stop when the reader submits the brief and source references. Record interruptions separately. Collect the brief before switching tasks. Hold feedback until both tasks are over.

| Participant | First task | Second task |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Apple, usual tools | Second company, Filing Lens |
| 2 | Apple, Filing Lens | Second company, usual tools |

This two-reader assignment covers each company under both workflows and alternates workflow order. Apple always comes first, so company and task position remain confounded. With a third participant, start with the second company and record the assignment before the session. With two or three readers, report individual outcomes and the effect of familiarity.
