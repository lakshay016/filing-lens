## Held-out companies (SPEC §7.7)

Alerts are judged on companies the classifier **never trained on**: companies with no
label in `eval/changes/labels.jsonl`. The author's holdings are not used (too few, not
representative).

`fl changes alert-sample --n 100 --seed 20261115`:

1. Held-out set = `t1_rag` companies with zero labels. If there are fewer than 8, stop
   and report: the author must add companies to `t1_rag` (ingest, parse, align) rather
   than reuse training companies.
2. For those companies, the classifier used is the final model of task 1.13, which by
   construction has not seen them. Run `rank_filing` for every eligible pair.
3. The sampling unit is the filing (needed for precision@5): draw filings at random
   (at most 3 per company) and take each drawn filing's top 5 text alerts by the
   **model** ranking and its top 5 by the **baseline** ranking (`1 - sim`, with
   `added`/`deleted` counted as `sim = 0`, ties by edge id), until the union reaches
   about 100 distinct alerts (20 filings give 100–200). Numeric alerts are not part of
   this evaluation.
4. Write `eval/changes/alert_queue.jsonl`: one line per distinct edge with its stable
   key (as in 1.13), the filing, and — **stored but never shown to the judge** — which
   ranking(s) selected it and at what rank. Order is shuffled with the seed.

## Judging page

`fl label alerts` → page `alerts.html`: one alert at a time, shown as the digest shows
it (Item, heading path, old and new text with word diff). Two buttons: `useful` /
`not useful` (keys `y` / `n`), plus an optional note. The page shows **no score, no
class, no rank and not which method selected the alert** (blind to the method).
Answers append to `eval/changes/alert_judgements.jsonl`
(`key`, `useful`, `judged_at`, `seconds`, `note`).

`eval/changes/ALERT_GUIDE.md`: "An alert is useful when a shareholder reading it learns
something about the company that they would not have known from last year's filing: a
new or removed risk, a changed fact, a changed commitment or exposure. Not useful:
wording refreshes, formatting, moved text, boilerplate adopted by many companies,
routine number updates." The author may refine this **before** judging starts, not
during.

## Relabel pass for κ (SPEC §7.3)

`fl changes relabel-sample --n 50 --seed 20261115`: refuses if fewer than 7 days have
passed since the latest pass-1 `labelled_at`. Draws 50 pass-1 labels at random,
stratified by annotation label, into `eval/changes/relabel_queue.jsonl`.
`fl label relabel` shows them with the task-1.13 page, **without the earlier label**,
and appends to `eval/changes/relabel.jsonl`.

## Metrics (`alert_eval.py`)

```python
def precision_shown(judgements, queue, method: str) -> tuple[int, int, float]
def precision_at_5_per_filing(judgements, queue, method: str) -> list[tuple[str, float]]
def bootstrap_over_filings(per_filing: Sequence[float], *, n_resamples=10_000, seed=20261115) -> tuple[float, float]
def cohens_kappa(a: Sequence[str], b: Sequence[str]) -> float
```

- Precision of shown alerts for a method = useful ÷ judged, over the alerts that method
  placed in a filing's top 5.
- Precision@5 per filing = share of that filing's top 5 (by the method) judged useful;
  a filing with fewer than 5 alerts uses what it has. Report the mean and a 95%
  bootstrap interval **over filings**.
- Report both methods side by side, and the paired difference (model − baseline) per
  filing with its bootstrap interval.
- Cohen's κ between pass 1 and pass 2 on the 50 relabelled edges, on the six annotation
  labels and again on the three training classes, with the raw agreement rate and the
  confusion table. Implement κ by hand (`(p_o − p_e) / (1 − p_e)`; return `1.0` when
  `p_e == 1`) and test against a worked example.
- An alert with no judgement makes `alert-eval` fail with the count missing; nothing is
  imputed.

`fl changes alert-eval [--write]` writes `eval/changes/results/{run_id}.json` and the
`alerts` section of `docs/RESULTS.md`, stating: number of alerts, filings and held-out
companies; single judge; judged blind to method; date; commit.

## Tests

- Precision and precision@5 on a hand-built queue with known answers (including an
  alert selected by both methods, and a filing with only 3 alerts).
- Bootstrap: deterministic; zero-width for constant input; resamples filings.
- κ: a worked 2×2 example (`p_o = 0.7`, `p_e = 0.5` → `0.4`), perfect agreement → 1,
  chance-level → about 0, a six-label example checked by hand.
- `relabel-sample` refuses before 7 days (freeze the clock).
- `alert-sample` excludes every company that has a label; fails when fewer than 8
  companies are held out.
- API payloads for both pages contain no score, class, rank, method or earlier label.
